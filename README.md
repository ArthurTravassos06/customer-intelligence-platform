# Customer Intelligence Platform

## Business Problem

The objective of this project is to build a customer intelligence platform capable of analyzing customer behavior, engagement, retention and product propensity.

The platform will support CRM decisions such as customer segmentation, churn prevention, cross-sell opportunities and Next Best Action.

## Main Questions

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which customers are at risk of churn?
- Which products are customers most likely to purchase?
- Which campaigns generate incremental conversion?
- What is the best action for each customer?
